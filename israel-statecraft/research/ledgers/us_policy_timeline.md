## Biden administration (Oct 2023 - Jan 2025)
- **2023-10-08/10**: Gerald R. Ford CSG to E. Mediterranean; Eisenhower CSG follows; "don't" deterrence message to Iran/Hezbollah. [H]
- **2023-10-18**: Biden wartime visit to Tel Aviv; $14.3B supplemental requested (passed Apr 2024 as ~$26B in $95B package). Bypassed Congress twice (Dec 2023) for emergency tank/artillery shells sales. [H]
- **2024-04-04**: After WCK aid-worker strike, Biden threatens conditioning; Erez opening. [H]
- **2024-04-13/14 & 10-01**: US leads coalition defense of Israel against both Iranian salvos; Oct 2024: THAAD battery + ~100 crew deployed to Israel. [H]
- **2024-05-08**: Biden pauses one shipment of 3,500 bombs (1,800 x 2,000-lb, 1,700 x 500-lb) over Rafah; "red line" speech. 2,000-lb hold persists to end of term (500-lb released July 2024). [H]
- **2024-05-31**: Biden announces 3-phase ceasefire framework (becomes UNSC Res 2735, June 10 2024). [H]
- **2024-Nov/Dec**: Post-election lame duck: Nov 26 Lebanon ceasefire brokered (Hochstein); Jan 2025 Gaza deal closed jointly by Biden (McGurk) and Trump (Witkoff) teams. [H]
