# Israeli Domestic Timeline (Oct 2023 - Aug 2026)

Compiled 2026-08-14. Confidence [H]/[M]/[L].

## Coalition & government
- **2023-10-11**: Emergency unity government; Gantz's National Unity joins; war cabinet (Netanyahu, Gallant, Gantz + observers Eisenkot, Dermer). Judicial overhaul frozen. [H]
- **2024-05-18**: Gantz ultimatum (day-after plan); **2024-06-09**: Gantz & Eisenkot quit; war cabinet dissolved June 17. Coalition back to 64 seats. [H]
- **2024-11-05**: Gallant fired (draft law + hostage-deal disputes); Katz to Defense, Sa'ar (rejoined Sept 2024 with 4 seats) to Foreign Ministry. Spontaneous protests. [H]
- **2025-01-21**: Ben Gvir's Otzma Yehudit (6 seats) quits over Gaza ceasefire; Smotrich stays after phase-2 assurances; **2025-03-19**: Ben Gvir returns as war resumes. [H]
- **2025-03 onward**: Netanyahu moves to fire Shin Bet chief Ronen Bar (HCJ freeze; Bar resigns, David Zini appointed) and against AG Baharav-Miara; "Qatargate" investigations of PM aides. [H]
- **2025-07-14/17**: UTJ then Shas quit the government over failure to legislate Haredi draft exemptions (Shas keeps coalition alignment, no portfolios). Trigger: HCJ June 2024 ruling requiring Haredi conscription + funding cuts; ~10-12k draft orders issued 2024-25, mass non-compliance, arrests of deserters. [H]
- **2025-10**: Shas conditions support for 2026 budget on draft-exemption law. Budget passes with patchwork (Dec 2025-Jan 2026). [M]
- **2026-05/06**: Netanyahu revives Haredi exemption bill; fails to secure votes ("Operation Roaring Lion"-era deferrals cited); UTJ boycotts coalition votes; coalition and Haredi parties converge on early dissolution. [H]
- **2026-06/07**: Knesset dissolution bill: preliminary reading 110-0, first reading 106-0; **Knesset dissolves ~July 17 2026**; **elections set for October 27 2026** (Sept date floated for holiday logistics; Oct 27 official). First ~full Knesset term since 1988. Netanyahu leads transition govt and seeks re-election; opposition consolidation (Bennett, Gantz, Lapid, Liberman, Eisenkot blocs) ongoing. [H date / M dynamics]

## Oct 7 accountability / state inquiry
- 2024: IDF internal probes; Halevi resigns (effective Mar 2025); Shin Bet probe (Bar takes responsibility). Netanyahu refuses wartime state commission of inquiry. [H]
- **2025-11-19**: HCJ conditional order: government must justify not forming a statutory state commission (Commissions of Inquiry Law: Supreme Court president appoints members). [H]
- **2025-12-15**: Likud private bill for a politically appointed "National-State Commission of Inquiry" (government picks members); government decides against a classic state commission. [H]
- **2026-07-06**: Knesset passes first reading of government-designed inquiry bill; opposition boycotts vote, calls body non-independent. As of Aug 2026: still NO independent state commission of inquiry, 3 years after Oct 7; issue central to election campaign. [H]

## Protests
- 2023: judicial-overhaul protest movement pivots to hostage-release advocacy (Hostages Families Forum; Kaplan/"Begin Gate" weekly rallies). [H]
- **2024-09-01**: After 6 hostages executed in Rafah tunnel: largest protests since war began (500k+ claimed); Histadrut general strike Sept 2. [H]
- 2025: March (Bar firing + resumed war = "unholy alliance" protests); August 2025: nationwide "day of stoppage" against Gaza City op; Oct 2025 ceasefire euphoria (Hostages Square). [H]
- 2026: protests over draft-exemption bill and inquiry-commission bill; election-season mobilization. [M]

## Reserve burden
- Oct 2023: ~300,000 reservists mobilized (largest ever call-up). [H]
- 2024-25: repeat tours of 150-300+ days; economic/family strain; falling turnout reported from spring 2025 (est. 60-75% vs >100% in 2023) as Gideon's Chariots extended service; refusal letters and pilot protests resurface; Haredi exemption resentment is the central political fault line. [M - IDF stopped publishing turnout figures]
- 2026: Iran war (Feb-Apr) re-mobilization incl. Home Front Command; reservist fatigue a top campaign issue. [M]

## Economy
- **2023-Q4**: GDP contracts ~19-20% annualized (~-5.6% q/q, construction/tourism collapse; 8% of workforce mobilized). 2023 growth 2.0%. [H]
- **Ratings downgrades (first in Israel's history)**: Moody's Feb 9 2024 A1->A2; S&P Apr 18 2024 AA- -> A+; Fitch Aug 12 2024 A+ -> A; Moody's Sept 27 2024 A2 -> Baa1 (negative); S&P Oct 1 2024 A+ -> A. [H]
- 2024: growth ~0.9-1%; deficit ~6.9% GDP; defense spending surges (~$28B+/yr, ~6% GDP structurally). [H]
- 2025: strong rebound: TASE at records, shekel +15% vs USD in 2025, tech FDI returns; deficit ~5%; growth ~2-2.5% (12-day war dent in Q2). Post-ceasefire: **Moody's and S&P revise outlook negative -> stable (late 2025 - early 2026), ratings unchanged (Baa1 / A)**. [H]
- 2026: IMF Article IV (Feb & Jul 2026): 2026 growth forecast cut to ~3.5% from 4.8% due to the Iran war (Finance Ministry had projected 5.1%); BoI holds rate (10th straight meeting; cuts begun Jan 2026 paused by war [M]); war costs (Home Front closures Feb-Apr, Ben Gurion capacity cuts) offset by reconstruction/rebound. Fiscal risk: defense ~6% GDP structural. [H]

- **2023-Q4 anchor numbers**: shekel hit 4.08/USD Oct 2023 then rallied; BoI sold $8.2B reserves; tourism -80%. [M]

## Emigration / brain drain
- TAU/CBS study (2026): Israelis leaving >=3 months: **86,509 (2023), 91,499 (2024), 90,922 (2025)** - three record years, 268,509 total vs 183,219 in 2013-15 (+50%). Net migration deficit two consecutive years (rare since founding). [H]
- Composition skews educated: 33% BA+ (vs 21.5% population), 23.5% MA (vs 11.9%); ~950 physicians left 2023-24 (net loss 500+ doctors, 2,360 engineers); 12% of Israeli-trained PhDs living abroad (2024 state report). [H]
- Sources: https://www.timesofisrael.com/record-israeli-emigration-continued-for-third-consecutive-year-in-2025-study-finds/ ; https://english.tau.ac.il/research/emigration-from-israel ; https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rkdmfjtlme
- Counterpoint: aliyah upticks from France/N. America 2024-25; return migration during 2026 war unclear. [L]

Outcome genuinely open. [M]
