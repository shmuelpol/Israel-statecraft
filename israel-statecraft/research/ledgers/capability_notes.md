## 2. Interceptor stocks / constraints
- Reporting during and after the June 2025 war (WSJ, others): Israel ran low on Arrow interceptors, rationing engagements and prioritizing populated areas; US THAAD battery in Israel expended an estimated 15-25% of the entire US THAAD inventory (~100-150 interceptors); US SM-3 use also drew down stocks. [M - consistent multi-outlet reporting, exact numbers classified]
- 2026 war: declining Iranian launch rates (attrition of launchers by air campaign) rather than interceptor exhaustion became the dominant dynamic; still, cluster-warhead salvos and leakage (~16 penetrations + ~50 cluster incidents in 40 days) reflected deliberate conservation and imperfect coverage. [M]
- Israel accelerated Arrow production and US resupply agreements 2025-2026; David's Sling took a larger share of engagements. [M]

## 3. Hezbollah arsenal
- Pre-war (Oct 2023) estimates: ~120,000-150,000 rockets/missiles incl. hundreds of precision-guided missiles (IDF/IISS/CSIS estimates). [H as estimate]
- Sept-Nov 2024 campaign: pager attacks (Sept 17-18) disabled thousands of operatives; IAF claims destruction of ~80% of medium/long-range launchers and most precision-missile project; Radwan force border infrastructure destroyed in ground op. Peak Hezbollah fire ~200-300 projectiles/day, far below pre-war projections of 3,000+/day. [M]
- Post-Nov 2024 ceasefire assessments: residual arsenal on the order of tens of thousands of short-range rockets but command, production and resupply (via Syria) badly degraded; loss of Syrian land bridge after Assad's fall (Dec 2024) cut rearmament. [M]
- 2025-2026: LAF confiscations south of Litani (phase 1); Hezbollah refuses disarmament north of Litani; limited retaliation capability shown Mar 2026 (small projectile fire after Khamenei killing). Qassem rejects June 2026 framework. [H]

## 4. Houthi / Red Sea
- Nov 19 2023: Galaxy Leader hijacked (crew held until Jan 2025). Nov 2023 - Dec 2024: 100+ attacks on shipping; Rubymar sunk (Mar 2024), Tutor sunk (Jun 2024); Red Sea transits fall ~60-70%; Suez revenue collapses. [H]
- Attacks on Israel: drone strike on Tel Aviv (Jul 19 2024, 1 killed) -> Israeli strike on Hodeidah port (Jul 20 2024, "Outstretched Arm"); ballistic missiles at Israel Sept 2024 - mid-2025 incl. Ben Gurion perimeter hit (May 4 2025) -> Israeli strikes on Hodeidah, Ras Isa, Salif, Sanaa airport (multiple, Dec 2024-Jun 2025). [H]
- US: Operation Prosperity Guardian (Dec 2023); Operation Rough Rider bombing campaign (Mar 15 - May 6 2025) ended in US-Houthi ceasefire (Oman-brokered). Houthis sank Magic Seas and Eternity C (Jul 2025, crew deaths/hostages). Attacks stopped after Oct 2025 Gaza ceasefire. [H]
- 2026: No new shipping attacks reported through mid-2026 (Security Council Report, S&P: carriers cautiously returning early 2026), BUT Houthis resumed missile/drone fire at Israel Mar 28 2026 joining the Iran war ("sensitive military sites", southern Israel); posture = conditional deterrence, capacity intact. Sources: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/monthly-forecast/2026-07/the-red-sea.php ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Houthi_strikes_on_Israel [H]

## 5. Iranian nuclear program
- Pre-June-2025: IAEA (May 31 2025 report) ~408 kg of 60% HEU (440.9 kg by the June 13 attacks per later ACA tally); total enriched stockpile >8,400 kg; IAEA Board finds Iran in NPT-safeguards non-compliance June 12 2025 (first in 20 years). [H]
- June 2025 strikes: Israel hit Natanz (above-ground PFEP destroyed, power cut to underground halls), Isfahan conversion facility, Arak/Khondab reactor, Tehran centrifuge workshops; US Operation Midnight Hammer (June 22 2025): 14 GBU-57 MOPs on Fordow + Natanz, Tomahawks on Isfahan. Damage assessments: initial DIA low-confidence "set back months" leak vs CIA/DOE/Israeli "severely damaged/destroyed, years"; IAEA Grossi: centrifuges at Fordow "no longer operational". Fate of the 60% stockpile (possibly moved pre-strike to Isfahan tunnels) unresolved. [H events / M damage]
- Post-June-2025: Iran suspends IAEA cooperation by law (Jul 2 2025); inspectors exit; Sept 2025 Cairo modalities agreement collapses when E3 trigger UN snapback sanctions (restored Sept 28 2025). Iran declares new underground enrichment site at Isfahan (June 2025) - never inspected; IAEA cannot verify (GOV/2026/8, Feb 27 2026). [H]
- Feb 28 2026 war: strikes on AEOI HQ, Qom/Isfahan targets, remaining program infrastructure; Iranian nuclear scientists again targeted. [M detail]
- As of Aug 2026: no IAEA access to enrichment sites; Grossi (Jun 24 2026): inspectors return only after a final deal per Iran; ~440 kg 60% HEU unaccounted for; US-Iran technical nuclear negotiations continue in parallel with hostilities (Oman channel, Aug 2026). Sources: https://www.armscontrol.org/factsheets/status-irans-nuclear-program-1 ; https://www.npr.org/2026/06/24/g-s1-129770/iran-nuclear-inspection ; https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/gov2026-8.pdf [H]

## 6. Hormuz campaign capabilities (2026)
- Iran closed the strait within 48h of Feb 28 2026 strikes: IRGC Navy fast-attack craft, mines, ASBMs/ASCMs, drones; "complete control" claimed Mar 4; Iran-approved tankers (China/India-bound) escorted through. [H]
- US response: CENTCOM GBU-72 deployment vs coastal silos (Mar 17); air campaign from Mar 19 (incl. A-10s/Apaches vs small craft); 16 boats destroyed at Bandar Lengeh (Mar 20); Israel killed IRGC Navy cdr Alireza Tangsiri (Mar 26); full US naval blockade of Iranian ports (Apr 12-15); Operation Project Freedom convoy/clearing (May 4, paused May 6). [H]
- Impact: 46 ships attacked, 14 seafarers killed by Jun 11 (IMO); ~1,000 vessels/20,000 crew stranded in Gulf 100+ days; oil price spike (Brent reportedly >$130 peak, sustained premium); Jun 17 Versailles MOU reopened strait toll-free ~Jun 17-Jul 8; traffic ~10 vessels/day vs 88-130 baseline as of Jul 30 2026; blockade reinstated Jul 14-15. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign [H; oil price [M]]
